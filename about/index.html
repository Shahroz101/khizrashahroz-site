Meet Khizra

Decorating should feel exciting—not overwhelming.

Hi, I'm Khizra. I believe a beautiful home doesn't have to be complicated, expensive, or perfectly styled all the time.

I'm here to share the decorating ideas, color combinations, cozy corners, and little details that can make your space feel more like you.

Whether you're refreshing one room or dreaming up an entirely new home: pretty rooms, practical ideas, and plenty of reasons to redecorate.

What you'll find here

Decorating guides organized room by room and idea by idea. Most posts are numbered lists you can skim in a few minutes and act on over a weekend; the rest are longer how-tos for the projects that need a plan.

How these guides are written

Every idea is illustrated, and photos are credited to their sources. I lean toward what's actually doable — one change at a time, in a real home, on a real budget — rather than rooms that only exist in showrooms.
