45 Christmas Bathroom Decor Ideas for a Cozy, Festive Holiday

Wreath-framed mirrors, plaid shower curtains, candlelit trays and tiny trees — 45 Christmas bathroom ideas from the vanity to the tub.

The bathroom is the last room most people decorate for Christmas, which is why it makes such a big impression when you do.

A guest washing their hands notices a few festive touches right away.

The trick is to keep it small and easy to swap: a towel set, a wreath on the mirror, a tray on the counter. Nothing has to be permanent, and nothing has to compete with your fixtures.

These 45 ideas cover the whole room, from mirrors and vanities to shelves, the toilet area, shower curtains and the tub.

How to Decorate a Bathroom Without Crowding It

Forty-five ideas is a lot, and no bathroom needs all of them.

Pick two or three and repeat a color, like red, gold or soft white, so the room feels like one picture.

Keep the counter clear enough to use, since this is still a working room.

Use battery candles and lights wherever water is near, and take everything down easily when the season ends.
