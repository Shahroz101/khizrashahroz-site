45 Christmas Bathroom Decor Ideas for a Cozy, Festive Holiday

Wreath-framed mirrors, plaid shower curtains, candlelit trays and tiny trees — 45 Christmas bathroom ideas from the vanity to the tub.

The bathroom is the last room most people decorate for Christmas, which is exactly why it makes such an impression when you do.

A guest washing their hands notices a few festive touches immediately, and a bathroom is small enough that you can finish the whole job in an evening.

It is also the room where decorating goes wrong most often, because of steam, splashes and a counter that has to keep working. So before the 45 ideas, here is how I approach it.

Five Rules for Decorating a Bathroom

Choose pieces you can swap out. Towels, soap dispensers, a wreath on the mirror. Nothing should be permanent, and nothing should fight your fixtures.

Keep the counter usable. If you can't set down a toothbrush, you have decorated too much.

Respect water and steam. Faux greenery lasts longer than real, and battery candles beat flames next to a sink or tub.

Repeat one color. Red, gold or soft white. If the same color shows up three times, the room looks planned.

Work in layers. Mirror, counter, shelf, floor. One touch at each level looks better than ten on the counter.

1. Start With the Mirror

The mirror is the biggest vertical surface in a bathroom, so it's where I always begin.

A frosted wreath with a red plaid bow hangs here, centered over the sink, while the little house and trees on the vanity sit off to one side. Symmetry up high, a looser arrangement below. That mix keeps a small room from feeling stiff.

Hang the wreath on a ribbon looped over the top edge of the mirror. No holes, no hooks, and it takes thirty seconds.

Keep it light. A heavy wreath will pull on the ribbon and tilt.

2. Repeat One Wreath in Two Places

Repetition is the cheapest way to make a room look planned. Two small berry wreaths, one on the mirror and one on the window, turn two separate surfaces into a pair. A lit garland along the back of the toilet adds a third layer of green without touching the counter.

Use removable hooks on glass and check the weight limit on the packet.

Two wreaths is plenty. A third and the room starts to feel like a shop.

4. Light It Low and Warm

Small bathrooms look best in soft, low light.

Fairy lights circle this oval mirror, candles glow on the cabinet and dark shelves of greenery sit above the toilet. The whole room feels like a cabin after dark.

A good rule: three light sources at different heights, one at the floor, one at the counter, one on the wall. That is what makes a room feel layered. Use battery lights and keep them out of the sink's splash zone. Warm white only, because cool white makes a bathroom look clinical.

Common Bathroom Mistakes (and Fixes)

Crowding the counter. Move half of it to a shelf or the window sill.

Using adhesive hooks for heavy wreaths. Steam softens the glue. Choose a ribbon loop or a screw-in hook.

Forgetting the floor. A non-slip Christmas rug does more for the mood than another figurine, and it's safer.

Putting real candles near towels and garland. Use battery ones and nobody has to worry.

Pick two or three ideas, repeat one color, and save the rest for next year. A small, tidy bathroom always beats a crowded one.
