44 Christmas Tiered Tray Ideas for a Festive Kitchen Counter

Cocoa bars, gingerbread bakeries, snowman families, buffalo plaid and lit villages — 44 Christmas tiered tray ideas to copy this season.

A tiered tray packs a lot of holiday cheer into a very small footprint.

It fits on a kitchen island, a counter corner or a console, and you can style it in an afternoon.

The best ones follow a single idea, such as a cocoa bar, a gingerbread bakery or a snowman family, instead of every Christmas item you own.

Height and texture do the rest: one tall piece like a mug or a mini tree, a few low fillers like garland and pinecones, and a sign or two to tie it together.

Here are 44 Christmas tiered tray ideas, grouped by theme, to copy or mix.

How to Style Your Own Christmas Tray

Pick one theme and one color pair, and let everything else go.

Put the tallest piece on the top tier, mid-height mugs and signs in the middle, and low fillers like garland, beads and pinecones along the edges.

Leave a little breathing room on each tier. A tray that is slightly under-filled always looks more styled than one that is crammed.

Then set it where you will see it every day, because that is the whole point.
