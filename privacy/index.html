Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

This site is a personal home decor blog. It collects as little information as it can while still working properly. This page explains what is collected, why, and the choices you have.

What this site collects

Analytics

khizrashahroz.com uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how visitors find the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects standard, non-identifying information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, the type of device and browser you use, and your approximate (city-level) location. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Email

If you email me through the contact page, I'll have your email address and whatever you choose to write. It's used only to reply to you and is never added to a mailing list or shared.

Nothing else

There are no accounts, no newsletter, no comments, and no forms that store information on this site.

Cookies

Cookies on this site come from two places:

  • Google Analytics cookies, used for the anonymous usage statistics described above.
  • Amazon cookies, which Amazon may set in your browser when you click an Amazon affiliate link on this site so that qualifying purchases can be credited. Those cookies are controlled by Amazon, not by this site. See the disclosure page for how affiliate links work here.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so won't stop the site from working.

Third parties

The services that handle data on this site's behalf are Google (analytics), Amazon (affiliate links you choose to click), and the web host that serves the site, which keeps standard server logs for security and reliability. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on or block analytics cookies in your browser.
  • Avoid clicking affiliate links if you'd rather Amazon didn't set a cookie — every product is also easy to find directly.
  • Email me to ask what, if anything, I hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Children

This site is written for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy on this site: khizra.sarwar@gmail.com.
