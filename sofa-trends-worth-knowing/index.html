20 Sofa Trends

From curved bouclé to deep modular sectionals — 20 sofa trends shaping how living rooms look and feel right now.

A sofa is usually the biggest piece in a living room, and it tends to stay put for years.

That makes it worth knowing which styles are shaping how sofas look and feel right now.

These 20 sofa trends cover the shapes, fabrics and colors showing up most — from curved bouclé to deep modular sectionals.

1. Modular Sectionals

Modular sectionals are built from separate sections that can be moved, added or rearranged as a room changes.

The chunky cream sofa here uses big, rounded sections and soft bouclé, which suits a room already full of shelves, plants and pattern.

It's an easy fit for anyone likely to move, or to rearrange the room often.

2. Curved Sofas

This half-circle cream sofa wraps around a round fluted coffee table, so the whole arrangement flows instead of feeling boxed in.

It works best where there's enough floor space for the curve to breathe.

Choosing the Trend That Fits

None of these 20 trends is universally right — the best one depends on the room's size, light and how the sofa will actually be used.

Whichever style wins, a well-chosen sofa remains one of the fastest ways to make a living room feel comfortable and finished.
