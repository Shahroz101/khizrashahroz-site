37 Winter Tray Ideas to Style a Cozy, Candlelit Home

Mini villages, cocoa bars, pinecones, brass candlesticks and frosted branches — 37 winter tray ideas for a coffee table, island or console.

A styled tray is the quickest way to make a coffee table, kitchen island or console feel ready for winter.

It's a small, contained project, so there's no need to redecorate a whole room, and it's easy to put away in January.

The trays that work best are built around one focal piece, whether that's a candle, a little village or a vase of greenery, with smaller details layered around it. Here are the rules I use, followed by 37 ideas.

Five Rules for a Winter Tray

Choose the tray first. Wood, metal and woven all set a different mood, and everything else builds on that.

Give it one focal point. A candle, a house, a tree or a vase. Everything else supports it.

Mix heights. Something tall, such as a candle or branch, something low, like pinecones or ornaments, and something soft, like greenery or ribbon.

Repeat a color. Two colors that show up at least twice each will make it look intentional.

Leave gaps. If you can't see the tray between objects, take something off.

Common Mistakes (and Fixes)

Too many small things. Group them or remove half.

Real candles near greenery. Use glass holders or battery candles.

A tray too small for the table. Choose one that is about a third of the table's width.

Forgetting function. If you move the tray every time you need the table, it's in the wrong place.

Keep it simple enough that you will enjoy looking at it all season. A tray you love beats a perfect one.
